# NextMinds redesign: design brief

Animation mode: animated-website

Design read: Ontario business owners and agents who want one partner for AI, web, real estate media and video. Register: calm, capable, quietly cinematic. Twenty years of craft, not a startup stunt.

Concept spine: The Aperture. Four disciplines (AI agents, web, real estate media, video) are four lens elements. Scroll aligns them into one clear aperture. The site reads like a lens being focused.

Delivery tier: cinema (Lenis smooth scroll, scroll-scrub film hero, motivated reveals).

Locked palette (user brand colors, explicit override of the near-black + cyan ban; ground is a deep teal-navy, not near-black, and the accent is the client's own brand cyan, used sparingly, no glows):
- ground #132C3C, deeper #0E2230, surface #1A394D, line #2B4D63
- ink #EAF4F8, muted #9DB6C4
- accent #00A1D6 (only accent)

Corner language: one soft 14px radius everywhere (cards, buttons, panels); circles only for step markers.

Locked type: Outfit (display and body) + IBM Plex Mono (labels, spec strips). Sans only.

Journey shape: single-shot.
Journey: one continuous push through four glass lens rings that drift from exploded to concentric and resolve into one clear aperture of cyan light. Hero chapter copy sits left over the dark negative space; the subject stays right of center.
World grammar: deep teal-navy seamless ground #132C3C, cool cyan rim light #00A1D6, polished glass faces with matte machined edges, fine light particles, locked exposure, no cuts, no shake, no text.
Mobile framing: subject center-safe, lighter mobile encode.
Delivery budget: desktop <= 32 MiB, mobile <= 16 MiB.

Section plan (one layout family each, no consecutive repeats):
1. Hero journey (scroll-scrub film + chapter copy + nav)
2. Services: interactive index list, big type rows, image reveals on hover (four rows)
3. About: asymmetric split, since 2001 statement + three plain facts (registered 2001, 20+ years, Ontario Canada)
4. Why choose us: four protocols as a divide-y ledger (Innovation, Project Management, Quality Assurance, Security)
5. Process: horizontal connected steps (Scalability, Latest AI tools, Quality Assurance)
6. Testimonials: one large rotating quote with names list (five real client quotes)
7. CTA: full-width closing panel
8. Footer

Asset plan: scroll film (+ mobile encode + posters), four service images (brand originals), logo, favicon, OG/cover.

CTA inventory (one label for the intent, "Start a Project"):
- Nav CTA: outlined rectangle that fills from the left on hover
- Hero primary: solid cyan block, label slides on hover
- Hero secondary "Our Services": text link with an arrow that travels
- Closing CTA: large pill with a rotating arrow disc
